# Brand

<p class="lede">So_ is the SoCLI mark. The rounded square is the terminal. So, in Geist Mono Bold, is the name. The capsule on that same baseline is the cursor.</p>

<div class="hero">
  <div class="hero-plate">

  </div>
  <p class="lede">The square we ship is black, with So and the cursor in <span class="token">#e7e9ea</span>, on a white ground. Twelve pixels of that ground stay empty around the square.</p>
</div>

## Color

<p>The square is black or white. The ground behind it is white, black, or clear. The cursor matches So, or it takes the product blue: <span class="token">#1d9bf0</span> on a black square and <span class="token">#1a8cd8</span> on a white one.</p>

## Permutations

<p>Put the black square on a light ground and the white square on a dark ground. A square the same color as its ground is included, and it will not read at a glance. The checkerboard stands in for a clear ground. It is not part of the mark.</p>

## Type

<p>This page is set in <a href="https://vercel.com/font">Geist</a>. The letters inside the mark stay <a href="https://vercel.com/font">Geist Mono</a> Bold. Geist is the text face. Geist Mono is the terminal face, which is why So is mono even when the sentence around it is not.</p>
